Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Short version: the app has no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no account. Your downloads and recordings stay on your PC. Information is only sent to me when you choose to contact support or buy Pro.

What stays on your PC

  • Settings (save folder, quality, recorder options) are kept in a settings.json file next to the app.
  • Support ID: a random code like 7F3K-92QD, created on your PC the first time the app runs. It isn't based on your name, hardware or anything about you. It only helps me find your message or licence when you contact me.
  • Your videos and recordings are saved only to the folder you choose. The app doesn't upload them anywhere.

What the app connects to

  • The websites you download from. yt-dlp contacts the site of the link you paste, like a browser would. If you turn on Browser login, yt-dlp reads that browser's cookies on your PC so the site sees you as logged in. The cookies are only sent to that site.
  • Tool installs and updates: winget (Microsoft) or the official GitHub releases of yt-dlp, FFmpeg and Deno, when you install or update them.
  • Microsoft Defender on your PC scans each finished file. That runs locally.

When you contact support

If you send the 💬 Support form, the app sends:

  • the subject, your email address and your message;
  • your support ID, the app version, your Windows version and the versions of yt-dlp, FFmpeg and Deno;
  • if you choose to include them: the app's recent log (which can contain links you pasted) and a screenshot.

The form is passed through a small Google Apps Script that emails it to my Gmail inbox (vteam732@gmail.com). The script keeps only a short-lived counter (cleared within an hour) to stop spam floods. If the form can't send, the app opens your own email app instead. Google processes these emails under Google's privacy policy.

I use support messages only to answer you and fix problems. I don't sell or share them. Ask me and I'll delete your messages.

When you buy Pro

  • The payment partner collects your name, email address, country and payment details to process the payment and send your licence key. Its own privacy policy applies to that.
  • I receive your name, email address, what you bought and when, and your licence key. I never see your card or UPI details.
  • When you activate or the app checks your licence, it sends your licence key and support ID to the payment partner's licence service, to confirm the key is valid and count the PCs it's used on.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see the GitHub privacy statement. The thank-you page reads your licence key from its own web address to show it to you; it doesn't send it anywhere.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions or deletion requests: vteam732@gmail.com.
